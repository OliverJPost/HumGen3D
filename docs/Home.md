#### *Welcome to the Human Generator documentation!*

Human Generator is an add-on for [Blender](https://blender.org) that makes it easy to add photorealistic humans to your projects. If you are looking for the store page and/or the trailer videos you can take a look at [our main website](https://humgen3d.com) or the [store page](https://blendermarket.com/products/humgen3d).

If you are looking for documentation about Human Generator, you are in the right place!

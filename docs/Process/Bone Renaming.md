> [!info] Part of the [[Process/Overview|Processing System]]

This processing option allows you to rename the bones of the rig according to a template.

![[Screenshot_Blender_000184.webp]]
1. **Suffix naming**: For bones that are mirrored (for example there is a left and right `upper_arm` bone) a suffix will be added. By default `.L` and `.R` is used, which gives `upper_arm.L` and `upper_arm.R`. Here you can define a different suffix to be added.
2. **Simple rename fields**: Fill in the name you want this bone to be renamed to. On the left you see the default HG naming for this bone.
3. **Mirrored rename fields**: The butterfly means this bone exists on both sides of the human. The suffix defined in (1) will be added to this bone name.

> [!tip] Want to reuse your naming template? Save it as a recipe ([[Process/Overview|Overview]]).

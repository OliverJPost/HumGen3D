### The face panel
![[Screenshot_Blender_000167.webp]]
1. The **randomize button** will pick random values for all sliders of the face. 
2. **Reset button**: This resets all sliders to their original 0.0 value.
3. **Slider categories**: The sliders are organized into categories. Want to change specific sliders? Click here and the category dropdown will open.
5. **Randomization lock**: Want to randomize the whole face, but keep some specific adjustments locked? Enabling this lock will prevent randomization of all sliders in this category.
6. **Randomize category button**: This will randomize only the sliders within this category. 

>[!info] Slider limits
>Want a stronger adjustment than possible with the slider? You can click and manually type in a value between -10.0 and +10.0 to go outside the slider limits.
>![[Screenshot_Blender_000165.mp4]]

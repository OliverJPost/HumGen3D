# Installation

Human Generator needs Blender 4.2 or newer. You install the add-on, and its setup installs the content. If something doesn't work, see [[Setup troubleshooting]].

> [!info] Installing the add-on zip that came with your Superhive order? That one installs differently, see [[How to install HG]].

## Install the add-on

1. Open your install guide on the computer you run Blender on: request the trial on [humgen3d.com](https://humgen3d.com) and press **Install right away**, or open the link in our email.
2. Press **Download Human Generator**.
3. Drag the downloaded zip into any Blender window and confirm with **OK**. Don't unzip it.

> [!tip]- Can't drag it, or the zip got unpacked?
> In Blender, go to Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions, open the menu at the top right and choose **Install from Disk**. Safari unzips downloads by itself: switch off *Open "safe" files after downloading* in its General settings, or use another browser.

## Install the free trial

The setup opens by itself. If it doesn't, press `N` in the 3D viewport, open the **HumGen** tab and press **Set up Human Generator**.

1. Press **Install the free trial**.
2. Press **Install**. The download is about 270 MB and runs in the background.
3. Press **Get started**.

The trial has one human, an outfit, a hairstyle and four poses. The rest of the library shows as locked.

## Install the full version

The full version comes as `.hgpack` files with your purchase. The setup installs them from your computer, without an account or a key.

1. Download the `.hgpack` files of your order. On Superhive they are under [Orders](https://superhivemarket.com/account/orders). Don't unzip them.
2. In Blender, press **I bought it**: in the *Trial version* box at the top of the HumGen tab, or on the first screen of the setup.
3. Press **Select .hgpack files…**, pick all of them and press **Install**.

The full library replaces the trial content. **8K Textures** is optional, and **HG Export** is what the Process tab needs.

Used Human Generator 4 before? The setup finds your content, so you don't install it again: see [[Setup troubleshooting#Content from an earlier install]].

## Updates

- **The add-on.** Its preferences show **Download the new version** when there is one. Download the zip and drag it into Blender, as you did the first time. It replaces the installed version and keeps your content and settings.
- **The content.** The HumGen tab shows *Content update available*. The trial updates by download. For the full version, download the newer `.hgpack` files from your purchase and install them as above.

## Blender version

On Blender 4.0 and 4.1 the add-on only shows a notice. Install Blender 4.2 or newer next to the one you have, where the setup finds your content again. Or stay on Human Generator **4.0.33**: in the add-on preferences press **Check now for humgen3d update**, then **Install main / old version**, and choose **v4.0.33**.

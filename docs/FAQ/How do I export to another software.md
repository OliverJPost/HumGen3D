---
description: How to export a Human Generator character from Blender to Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, Mixamo or any other program.
---
Use the **Process** tab of Human Generator. Select the human, pick the recipe of the program you export to, and press the export button. The hair is converted to hair cards, the materials are baked to textures and the rig becomes a game skeleton with the right bone names, all automatically.

- [[Process/Overview|The Process guide]] explains the tab and every option.
- Step-by-step guides per program: [[Export to Unity]], [[Export to Unreal Engine]], [[Export to Godot]] and [[Export to Mixamo]].
- For a program that is not listed, use the *Generic game* recipe, and pick the [[Skeleton|bone names]] and [[Bake Textures|texture packing]] it expects.
